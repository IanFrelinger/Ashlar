## Friend short

Turns useful AI **recipes** into **checked, reusable building blocks** — instead of improvising from scratch every time.

## Friend long

Ashlar isn't another chatbot. It's a **bouncer + receipt** system: **check the recipe before it runs**, **package** what's approved, **record** what happened, and **reuse** it elsewhere instead of reinventing.

## 15-second (warm)

Ashlar isn't a chat box. It's a control layer that turns AI know-how into **verified behavior** — receipts for what ran, and a bouncer so new **plugins/recipes** get **checked before they run**. You can reuse that behavior elsewhere and still stay in control.

## 30-second (warm)

Most AI tools are a chat window. Ashlar embeds under your product: check AI-assisted behaviors before they run, package what's admitted, leave a receipt, and re-check at the next host. Models can change; the **behavior lives in the contract**. Local-first. The AI suggests; the company keeps the keys.

**Design partner (~$4k / 90 days):** one AI-assisted behavior you already rely on → admission gate → package for reuse → auditable receipts.
