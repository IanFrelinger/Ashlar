SPEC-007 PR 4.3: every redirect hop is decided before it is sent (R-a, R-b, R-c; D32, D33; Q5)

An internal redirect follower, EgressRedirectHandler, sits directly above the primary handler of
every EgressHttp client and handler and of every IHttpClientFactory client under AddAshlarEgressGuard
(the host's clients included, owner answer Q5). It turns an HttpClientHandler's or
SocketsHttpHandler's own AllowAutoRedirect off, remembers the original per instance, follows as the
runtime would (300/301/302/303/307/308, the primary's limit, relative Location resolved, fragment
kept, never https to http, method rewrite on 301/302/303, Authorization cleared) and decides each
new authority (scheme, host, port) with the guard handler's guard, family and site before the hop is
sent. A handler between the guard handler and the primary that rewrites RequestUri is decided again
(EgressHopEvaluation, a per-request note in HttpRequestMessage.Options; Properties on ns2.0).
Factory clients follow across origins (P1); EgressHttp clients return a cross-origin 3xx (P2).
EgressRedirectFilter (an IHttpMessageHandlerBuilderFilter registered by AddAshlarEgressGuard) puts
the guard handler back, with a Warning (7303), when a client's configuration removed it, and on
AirGapped/SecureWorkstation names a primary type whose following cannot be turned off (7304). A
primary of another type, with Credentials, or already started keeps following on its own and the
final authority is decided after the send. On netstandard2.0 the follower sits under the
synchronous-send hop, whose Inner the follower's walker steps through.

By the owner's decision of 2026-10-06 (O2, SPEC-007 decisions log) a hop from outside the host
boundary into it (loopback, localhost and *.localhost, unix, npipe, link-local) is never followed on
either route: the hop is recorded and the 3xx returned, so a remote peer cannot bounce a request into
a local service. A caller's token cancelled between hops stops the follower before the next hop is
sent or decided; intermediate responses are disposed; a Wrap over a chain with no primary yet only
decides (Known limit).

Both SNS signing-certificate clients (API and Fleet.Host) never follow, through the new
EgressHttpClientBuilderExtensions.NeverFollowRedirects (configure-existing overload); the Bedrock
runtime client's configuration never follows (AwsBedrockChatClientFactory.RuntimeConfig).

Still report-only: a hop the guard would refuse is decided Refused before it is sent, and still sent
until PR 4.7. Behaviour changes in the CHANGELOG. Known limits (docs/EgressInventory.md): only
Authorization is cleared on a hop; a cloning handler costs a duplicate decision; a primary of another
type, with credentials, already started, or attached after the wrap is decided after the send.
